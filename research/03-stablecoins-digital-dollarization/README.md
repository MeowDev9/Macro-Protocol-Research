# Stablecoins and Digital Dollarization

Research supporting Macro Protocol's Week 3 analysis of how stablecoins are expanding access to the U.S. dollar and beginning to interact with traditional foreign-exchange markets.

## Research Question

Are stablecoins creating a new monetary system, or are they giving the existing dollar system a new distribution network?

## Key Findings

- Roughly **98% of stablecoin value is denominated in U.S. dollars**.
- In Nigeria, stablecoins represented **more than 65% of crypto inflows in 2024**.
- Nigeria accounted for roughly **60% of sub-Saharan African stablecoin inflows** from late 2019 to early 2025.
- Recent research finds measurable spillovers from stablecoin inflows into traditional FX markets, although the estimated effects remain small.
- Stablecoins may therefore be better understood as a new access and distribution layer for dollar-linked value rather than a replacement for the dollar itself.

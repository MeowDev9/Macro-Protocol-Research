# RWA Concentration Risk

Supporting research for Macro Protocol's analysis of concentration in the tokenized U.S. Treasury market.

This repository contains the methodology, sources, data, and supporting material behind the research note:

**RWA Concentration Risk: What the Tokenized Treasury Data Actually Shows**

## Key Findings

- Tokenized U.S. Treasuries were roughly **$16B** by late August 2026.
- The **top 5 products held about two-thirds** of the market.
- The **top 10 held about 88%**.
- BUIDL's market share declined from its earlier peak as competing products gained share.
- On-chain distribution does not necessarily mean institutional decentralization.

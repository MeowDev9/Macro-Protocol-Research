# Methodology

This research uses a late-August 2026 snapshot of the tokenized U.S. Treasury market.

## Market Scope

- **RWA.xyz** is used for distributed on-chain value and tokenized Treasury market size.
- **Token Terminal** is used only as a separate cross-reference for tokenized funds and BUIDL market-share history.
- Figures from RWA.xyz and Token Terminal are not treated as directly interchangeable because their category definitions differ.

## Concentration

Top 5 and Top 10 concentration were calculated from the RWA.xyz Treasury product listing:

- **Top 5 concentration** = combined value of the five largest Treasury products / total Treasury market value
- **Top 10 concentration** = combined value of the ten largest Treasury products / total Treasury market value

Rounded results:

- Top 5: approximately two-thirds
- Top 10: approximately 88%

## BUIDL Market Share

BUIDL market share was calculated as:

BUIDL market value / total market value within the relevant dataset

Historical and current shares are treated as dated snapshots, not permanent values.

## Notes

All market figures are time-sensitive.

Where datasets use different classification methodologies, they are shown separately rather than merged.
